How we price our work.

We consider the nature of the assignment, the work involved, its complexity and the resources required.

For projects, we provide a clear quotation based on agreed deliverables. For ongoing services, we agree a monthly fee reflecting the responsibilities and support required.

Any additional costs, such as specialist services or software, are discussed and agreed in advance.

Questions about pricing.

Are the prices shown fixed?
No. Monthly prices are starting rates. The final fee depends on the scope, complexity, expected workload and level of support required, and is agreed before work begins.
Can I engage Brayden Partners for a single assignment?
Yes. You can work with us on a one-off assignment, a defined project or on an ongoing basis.
Do I need to commit to a monthly contract?
Not for one-off or project-based work. Ongoing HR support is provided under an agreed monthly arrangement.
What is the difference between HR Advisory and Outsourced HR Management?
With HR Advisory, you carry out your own HR activities and we provide professional guidance. With Outsourced HR Management, we take responsibility for agreed recurring HR tasks on your behalf.

All prices are in GBP. Brayden Partners is not VAT registered, so no VAT is added to our fees.
