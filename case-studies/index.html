Building a people function from zero across multiple countries

An early-stage energy finance startup scaling from 2 to 80+ employees, with expansion into multiple international markets.

+3,900%Workforce growth supported
+40%Onboarding efficiency improvement
+60%Process consistency improvement

  • Built the entire people function from the ground up
  • Designed onboarding, performance and employee lifecycle processes
  • Established organisational structure and role clarity
  • Implemented systems and people data tracking
  • Developed scalable frameworks for multi-country operations
  • Supported expansion into new international markets
Key outcomes

  • Compliant, smooth international expansion

After

  • Expansion-ready operating model
