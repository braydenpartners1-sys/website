Business Operations & Structure

Build a business that runs effectively.

As businesses grow, informal ways of working can become difficult to manage. Responsibilities overlap, decisions depend on a few individuals and routine activities lack clear procedures.

We help businesses establish practical operating structures, improve workflows and organise responsibilities so that work can be managed more effectively.

How we can help
Business operating structures
Define departments, functions, reporting arrangements and responsibilities.
Standard operating procedures
Develop documented procedures for routine business activities.
Process mapping and improvement
Review how work is completed, identify inefficiencies and improve workflows.
Roles and responsibilities
Develop role profiles, reporting lines and accountability arrangements.
Operational planning
Establish practical plans, priorities and management routines.
Business setup support
Help new businesses put essential internal operating arrangements in place.

What you can expect. Clearer responsibilities, documented processes and practical operating arrangements suited to your business.

People & HR Advisory

Get the people side of your business right.

Employing people brings responsibilities that require more than informal arrangements. Businesses need clear employment terms, fair processes, effective managers and appropriate systems for managing their workforce.

We provide practical HR advice and support for businesses establishing their people practices or improving existing arrangements.

How we can help
Employment contracts and documentation
Prepare employment contract templates, employee handbooks and related documentation.
HR policies and procedures
Develop policies covering absence, leave, conduct, grievance, performance and other employment matters.
Recruitment and onboarding
Establish recruitment procedures, interview frameworks, pre-employment checks and induction processes.
Employee relations
Support employers with workplace concerns, disciplinary matters, grievances, absence and performance issues.
Performance management
Develop objectives, review processes, manager guidance and practical performance frameworks.
Workforce planning and organisation design
Review staffing requirements, reporting structures and role responsibilities.
HR process improvement
Simplify administrative processes, improve employee records and strengthen day-to-day HR delivery.
Ongoing HR advisory
Provide agreed ongoing support for businesses without a dedicated internal HR function.

What you can expect. Practical employment documentation, clearer people-management processes and professional HR support appropriate to your workforce.

Governance & Business Processes

Strengthen how your business is managed.

Good governance is not only for large organisations. Every business benefits from knowing who makes decisions, how responsibilities are assigned and what controls are needed to manage its operations.

We help businesses review and improve their internal management arrangements, with an emphasis on practical governance rather than unnecessary bureaucracy.

How we can help
Governance frameworks
Establish clear management responsibilities, reporting arrangements and decision-making processes.
Internal policies and procedures
Develop operational policies and management procedures suited to the organisation.
Delegation and approvals
Define decision-making authority, approval limits and accountability.
Operational risk reviews
Identify risks within existing business processes and recommend proportionate controls.
Compliance processes
Help businesses organise internal procedures, responsibilities and records for meeting applicable obligations.
Management reporting
Establish reporting arrangements that provide useful information for business oversight.
Business process reviews
Examine existing procedures, identify control gaps and recommend improvements.

What you can expect. More clearly defined responsibilities, practical internal controls and better-documented management arrangements.

Where specialist legal, financial or regulated advice is required, we work within our professional scope and engage or recommend appropriately qualified specialists.
