HR Foundations

The right foundations from your first hire.

Hiring your first employees brings legal duties and people processes that need to be in place from day one. Getting them right early avoids problems that are much harder to fix later.

We help startups and early-stage businesses set up the essentials of an HR function, in proportion to their size.

Our work may include
  • Employment contracts and written statements of terms
  • Core HR policies and a practical employee handbook
  • Right to work checks, onboarding and probation processes
  • Organisational structure and role definitions
  • Choosing and setting up a simple HR system
  • Employee records and baseline people reporting
What clients receive

A compliant, practical HR foundation that is ready to grow with the business.

HR Transformation

Make your HR function work better.

As organisations change, HR processes can become fragmented, responsibilities unclear and administrative demands increasingly difficult to manage.

We help organisations review and improve how HR services are delivered.

Our work may include
  • Reviewing existing HR processes and service delivery arrangements
  • Mapping workflows and identifying duplication, delays and unnecessary administration
  • Redesigning processes for recruitment, onboarding, employee records and other HR activities
  • Clarifying responsibilities, approvals and decision-making authority
  • Supporting restructuring and the implementation of new working arrangements
  • Developing documentation and supporting managers through change
What clients receive

Clearer processes, defined responsibilities, practical implementation plans and improved ways of working.

Lean HR Operating Models

The right HR structure for your business.

Not every organisation needs a large HR department or complex processes. What matters is having the right responsibilities, systems and support in place.

We design HR operating models that reflect your size, workforce and business needs.

Our work may include
  • Assessing existing HR responsibilities and capacity
  • Defining HR roles, accountabilities and reporting lines
  • Designing how HR services are delivered
  • Establishing approval processes and decision-making responsibilities
  • Developing practical policies, procedures and management tools
  • Identifying where administration can be simplified and overhead reduced
What clients receive

An HR operating structure with clear responsibilities, workable processes and a practical approach to delivering people services.

People Systems & Automation

Less manual work. Better-connected processes.

Manual spreadsheets, disconnected systems and repeated administrative tasks consume time and make workforce information difficult to manage.

We help organisations improve their HR systems and introduce automation where it adds practical value.

Our work may include
  • Reviewing existing HR systems and administrative workflows
  • Identifying opportunities for automation
  • Supporting HR software selection and implementation
  • Designing digital workflows for routine people processes
  • Improving employee data structures and record management
  • Supporting configuration, testing, documentation and user adoption
What clients receive

More efficient workflows, better-organised workforce information and systems that are easier to use and maintain.

People Analytics & Workforce Intelligence

Make better decisions with reliable workforce information.

Organisations collect a great deal of employee data, but it is not always structured or presented in ways that support decisions.

We help businesses build practical approaches to workforce reporting and analysis.

Our work may include
  • Reviewing existing workforce data and reporting
  • Defining useful HR metrics and reporting requirements
  • Developing workforce dashboards and management reports
  • Improving data quality and reporting processes
  • Analysing trends in absence, turnover and recruitment
  • Setting up reporting that supports workforce planning and operational decisions
What clients receive

Reliable workforce reporting, clearer management information and better visibility of workforce trends.
