HR Foundations

Get the essentials in place.

Employing people creates responsibilities that need clear documentation, consistent processes and appropriate management arrangements.

For a new or growing business, the challenge is knowing what needs to be established now and what can develop as the organisation grows.

We help businesses put the necessary HR foundations in place without building an unnecessarily complicated function.

What we can help with
Employment documentation
Employment contracts, offer letters, contractual variations and other essential employee documents.
HR policies and procedures
Development of core employment policies, management procedures and supporting guidance appropriate to the organisation.
Recruitment and onboarding
Recruitment workflows, selection documentation, pre-employment checks, induction arrangements and onboarding processes.
Employee records
Processes for maintaining employee information, managing documentation and establishing appropriate recordkeeping arrangements.
Probation and performance
Probation frameworks, review documentation, objective-setting processes and practical guidance for managers.
HR compliance
Reviews of existing arrangements against relevant employment obligations, with specialist legal input where required.

What you receive. Depending on the agreed scope, this may include employment documents, policy manuals, process maps, templates, management guidance and implementation support.

Who this is for. Businesses employing staff for the first time, organisations formalising their HR arrangements and growing companies that need more structured people management processes.

HR Transformation

Improve how HR works.

HR functions often develop around immediate business needs.

Over time, this can lead to duplicated activities, inconsistent processes, unclear responsibilities and administrative work that takes longer than necessary.

We help organisations review how HR services are delivered and identify what needs to change.

What we can help with
HR process reviews
Examination of recruitment, onboarding, employee changes, absence administration, reporting and other employee lifecycle activities.
Service delivery improvement
Review of how employees and managers access HR support, how requests are handled and where work becomes delayed.
Process redesign
Development of clearer workflows, responsibilities, approval arrangements and supporting documentation.
Organisational change
Support with the people and operational aspects of restructuring, changes to responsibilities and implementation planning.
HR technology alignment
Assessment of whether existing systems support the required processes and identification of improvements.
Change implementation
Support with communication, documentation, manager guidance and the introduction of revised arrangements.

What you receive. A documented assessment of the current position, agreed improvement priorities, redesigned processes and implementation support. The precise deliverables depend on the size and complexity of the engagement.

Lean HR Operating Models

Organise HR around what the business needs.

Not every organisation needs the same HR structure.

Some businesses need a dedicated HR team. Others may be better served by a combination of internal responsibilities, external support and suitable technology.

The important question is how HR work should be organised so that responsibilities are clear and support is available when needed.

What we can help with
HR function design
Assessment of the activities the organisation needs its HR function to deliver.
Roles and responsibilities
Definition of responsibilities across HR, line managers, leadership and external providers.
Decision-making arrangements
Clear accountability for recruitment, workforce changes, approvals, escalation and employee management decisions.
Service delivery models
Design of internal, outsourced or blended HR support arrangements.
Capacity and resource planning
Assessment of workload, service requirements and available resources.
Operating procedures
Documentation of key workflows, reporting lines and service expectations.

What you receive. An HR operating model describing the proposed structure, responsibilities, service arrangements and supporting processes.

HR Systems & Technology

Make routine work easier to manage.

Many organisations rely on spreadsheets, email chains and manual updates to manage important workforce activities.

These arrangements can become difficult to maintain as employee numbers and operational requirements increase.

We help organisations examine existing workflows and determine where better systems or automation could improve how work is managed.

What we can help with
Workflow reviews
Mapping existing administrative activities to identify delays, repeated tasks and unnecessary manual steps.
System requirements
Defining what a new or improved system needs to do before selecting technology.
Software selection
Assessment of suitable platforms against business requirements, costs and implementation considerations.
Process automation
Automation of appropriate notifications, approvals, document workflows and administrative activities.
Implementation support
Assistance with configuration, process documentation, testing and user adoption.
System improvement
Review of existing tools to identify functionality that is underused or poorly aligned with business processes.

What you receive. Depending on the project, this may include workflow maps, requirements documents, system recommendations, configured processes, automation solutions and user guidance.

People Analytics & Workforce Intelligence

Make better decisions with workforce information.

Workforce information is often spread across HR systems, payroll records, spreadsheets and departmental reports.

The challenge is bringing that information together in a way that helps managers understand what is happening and where attention is needed.

We help organisations improve the quality, structure and use of their workforce data.

What we can help with
Workforce reporting
Development of useful measures covering headcount, absence, turnover, recruitment and other relevant workforce activities.
Data quality
Review of data sources, definitions and reporting processes to improve consistency and reliability.
Management dashboards
Development of dashboards that present workforce information clearly and support regular management reviews.
Workforce cost analysis
Reporting that helps organisations understand workforce costs, staffing patterns and resourcing requirements.
Recruitment intelligence
Analysis of recruitment activity, vacancies, hiring stages and related operational information.
Reporting processes
Establishment of responsibilities, reporting cycles and guidance for interpreting workforce information.

What you receive. Agreed reporting measures, structured datasets, management reports, dashboards and supporting documentation.

AI Adoption

Use AI where it makes business sense.

AI offers new ways to handle information, support administrative work and improve business processes.

But introducing AI without understanding the underlying work can create additional costs, risks and complexity.

We help businesses identify suitable applications, test them and establish appropriate arrangements for their use.

What we can help with
AI opportunity assessments
Review of business workflows to identify suitable applications for AI and automation.
Use case evaluation
Assessment of feasibility, cost, operational value, data requirements and potential risks.
Workflow pilots
Development and testing of selected AI-enabled workflows.
Tool selection
Assessment of appropriate AI platforms and integration requirements.
Employee adoption
Practical guidance and training to help employees understand and use approved tools.
AI governance
Development of usage guidance, oversight arrangements and appropriate safeguards.

What you receive. An opportunity assessment, implementation roadmap, working pilot or adoption framework, depending on the agreed engagement.
