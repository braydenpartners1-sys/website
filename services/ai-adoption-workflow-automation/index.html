AI Adoption & Workflow Automation

Make AI work for your business.

AI creates opportunities to improve how organisations operate, but introducing another tool doesn’t automatically make work easier or more efficient. We help businesses identify practical applications for AI, assess their readiness and implement solutions that fit their existing operations.

Discuss AI opportunities
Our approach

Start with the problem, not the technology.

Not every business process needs AI.

Sometimes a clearer workflow, a better system or straightforward automation is the more effective solution. We review how work is currently done, identify where improvements are needed and assess whether AI can deliver meaningful value.

Our focus is on practical implementation, responsible adoption and solutions that employees can confidently use.

What we help with

Practical AI across the business, not just HR.

Typical areas include AI-assisted document processing, internal knowledge search, AI-supported administrative workflows, reporting and information retrieval, and everyday productivity tools.

Our work may include
  • Reviewing business processes to identify AI and automation opportunities
  • Assessing existing tools, information and operational readiness
  • Prioritising use cases based on business value, feasibility, cost and risk
  • Supporting the selection and configuration of suitable AI tools
  • Designing AI-assisted administrative and operational workflows
  • Piloting solutions and evaluating their effectiveness
  • Training employees and managers to use AI responsibly
  • Developing practical guidance, controls and human oversight arrangements

Looking for HR-specific technology, such as an HR system or onboarding workflows? See People Systems & Automation.

Ways to work with us

How we can help you get started.

You don’t need to know which AI tools to use or have a detailed plan. We help you identify where AI makes sense, test practical solutions and introduce them into everyday operations. The three options follow a natural order, from assessing to piloting to adopting, but each is available on its own.

1. Assess

AI Opportunity Assessment

Find out where AI could make a difference.

We review your existing processes and workflows to identify opportunities for AI and automation. A practical starting point if you are interested in AI but unsure where to begin.

What you get
  • Review of relevant business processes and existing tools
  • Identification and prioritisation of potential AI use cases
  • Assessment of feasibility, costs, benefits and risks
  • A practical implementation roadmap
Ask about an assessment
2. Pilot

AI Workflow Pilot

Start with one problem. Build a working solution.

Rather than introducing AI across your whole organisation, we focus on one agreed workflow, with clear boundaries and success criteria, and test whether AI or automation improves it.

What you get
  • A clearly defined use case and success criteria
  • Configuration and implementation of suitable tools
  • A working pilot with testing and user feedback
  • Evaluation of results and recommendations for wider adoption
Ask about a pilot
3. Adopt

AI Adoption & Governance

Help your people use AI confidently and responsibly.

Introducing AI is only part of the challenge. Employees need to know how to use it well, what information they can share and when human judgement is required.

What you get
  • Practical AI usage policies and guidance
  • Employee and manager training
  • Data protection, security and risk considerations
  • Human oversight and approval arrangements
  • Recommendations for ongoing AI governance
Ask about adoption support

Not sure where AI fits into your business?

Start with the problem, not the technology. Tell us how your organisation works today and where you see room for improvement, and we’ll help you assess whether AI, automation or a simpler process change is the right answer.
