Where AI can help: look at the work before choosing the tool.

AI can help businesses process information, manage routine activities and support employees with certain types of work. The opportunity is not simply to introduce AI tools. It is to identify where they can make a useful difference and whether they are appropriate for the organisation.

Potential applications include document processing, internal information retrieval, routine correspondence, reporting support and administrative workflows.

The right solution depends on the nature of the work, the information involved, existing systems and the level of human oversight required.

In some cases, conventional automation or process redesign may be more suitable than AI.

Three ways to start with AI.

AI Opportunity Assessment

Understand where AI could add value.

We review selected business processes and identify opportunities where AI or automation may improve the way work is carried out.

The engagement covers
  • Review of existing workflows and tools
  • Identification of potential AI use cases
  • Assessment of feasibility and implementation requirements
  • Consideration of costs, benefits and risks
  • Prioritisation of opportunities
  • Development of an implementation roadmap

What you receive. A documented assessment of potential applications, their suitability and recommended next steps.

AI Workflow Pilot

Test one use case before committing to wider adoption.

A pilot allows an organisation to examine whether a proposed solution works in its actual operating environment.

We focus on a defined workflow, establish success criteria and develop a solution that can be tested with the people who will use it.

The engagement covers
  • Definition of the workflow and problem
  • Selection of appropriate tools
  • Configuration and implementation
  • Testing with agreed users
  • Review of outputs, reliability and risks
  • Evaluation against agreed success criteria

What you receive. A tested pilot, supporting documentation and recommendations on whether and how to proceed.

AI Adoption & Governance

Introduce AI with clear expectations and safeguards.

Employees need to understand which tools are approved, how they should be used and when human review is necessary.

Organisations also need to consider confidentiality, data protection, security and accountability.

The engagement covers
  • Review of intended AI use
  • Development of internal AI usage guidance
  • Employee and manager training
  • Consideration of data protection and security risks
  • Human oversight and approval arrangements
  • Recommendations for ongoing monitoring and governance

What you receive. An agreed adoption and governance framework, supporting guidance and relevant training materials.
